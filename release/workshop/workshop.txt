description=Put KI5 on Kentucky's roads.
description=
description=KI5-only world traffic, profession vehicles, regional police liveries, roadside stories, consistent military camo and military vehicle zones for Project Zomboid Build 42.21. Community integration by Erika (prg299), based on the original unlisted Vehicle Suite.
description=
description=[b]Release candidate 0.3.1: native gameplay and multiplayer results and remaining release checks are documented on GitHub. Workshop publication is pending.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]Included integrations[/h2]
description=More Car Variants, Consistent Camo, Lore-Friendly Police Skins with the B42 hotfix, Vehicle Military Zones, and No Vanilla selection/duplicate-suppression behavior. All four military road-story recipes use internal authored native game helpers for zones, angled parking, scene construction and scheduling. YAPZLib is not required or bundled. Six stale MCV vehicle definitions are omitted so current KI5 definitions take priority. All original authors retain ownership of their contributions.
description=
description=[h2]Requirements and load order[/h2]
description=Subscribe to the listed original KI5 vehicle requirements plus [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3171167894]that DAMN Library[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3520758551]More Car Features + Spawn Zones Expansion[/url]. These are the only non-vehicle dependencies. Enable them before OnlyKI5VehicleSpawnLiveryOverhaul. They remain external and are not bundled. Keep YAPZLib only if another mod needs it.
description=
description=Disable the separately installed MCV modules, KI5Camo/PapaChadCamo, LFPSP and LFPSP-B42Fix, VMZNEW, No Vanilla selection modules, and the old Vehicle Suite/World Vehicles/Regional Police Skins integrations when using this mod. They are already integrated or replaced here and would duplicate assets or registrations. Fresh chunks are needed for changed spawns; existing vehicles are not rewritten.
description=
description=[h2]Silent police lightbars[/h2]
description=Eligible newly spawned police vehicles have a 50% flashing-lightbar chance in single-player and multiplayer, outside dedicated emergency-station parking. The spawn policy clears siren modes before checking existing lights or saved spawn decisions. Automatic flashing lights must stay silent.
description=
