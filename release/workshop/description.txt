[h1]Only KI5: Vehicle Spawn & Livery Overhaul[/h1]
Put KI5 on Kentucky's roads. Regional police, military checkpoints and roadside scenes.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]KI5 traffic.[/b] Replaces vanilla traffic, profession vehicles and roadside vehicle choices.
[*] [b]Local police.[/b] Regional liveries for Louisville, Muldraugh, West Point and other jurisdictions.
[*] [b]Silent lightbars.[/b] Eligible police spawns have a 50% flashing-light chance outside emergency parking. Lights on, siren off, in single-player and multiplayer.
[*] [b]Military encounters.[/b] Military zones, checkpoint fleets and four roadside stories with consistent camo.
[*] [b]More variety.[/b] Integrated vehicle variants, police skins and duplicate suppression.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[b]Build 42.21[/b], the listed original KI5 vehicle packs, damnlib and More Car Features / WayMoreCars. Enable requirements before this mod.

[b]YAPZLib is not required.[/b] Internal helpers replace it.

[b]Highly recommended:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3808100388]KI5 Vehicle Performance Patch (Unofficial)[/url] by lucygoosey. Optional; follow its server/client setup instructions.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Compatibility & saves[/h2]
Changes appear in [b]fresh chunks[/b]; existing vehicles are not rewritten. Back up saves before changing mods.

Disable standalone MCV, Consistent Camo, police skins/B42 hotfix, VMZ, No Vanilla selection mods and the old Vehicle Suite integrations: their selected content is already included.

[b]0.3.1 unlisted test release.[/b] Single-player, two-client multiplayer, silent lightbars and save/reload have been checked. Further acceptance testing continues.
