[h1]Only KI5: Vehicle Spawn & Livery Overhaul[/h1]
Put KI5 on Kentucky's roads.

KI5-only world traffic, profession vehicles, regional police liveries, roadside stories and military-fleet tuning for Project Zomboid Build 42.21. Independent community addon by Erika (prg299).

[b]Release candidate 0.1.0: native gameplay and multiplayer acceptance pending.[/b]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
This is a full-fleet integration. Subscribe to and enable all listed required items and modules before OnlyKI5VehicleSpawnLiveryOverhaul. All original vehicle, variant, camo and police-skin content stays in its original mods. No KI5 or third-party asset repack.

Use the police B42 hotfix instead of original LFPSP. Do not enable the private Vehicle Suite, World Vehicles, Regional Police Skins or No Vanilla selection modules alongside this addon. Fresh chunks are needed for changed spawns; existing vehicles are not rewritten.
