[h1]ONLY KI5: Vehicle Spawn & Skins[/h1]
Put KI5 on Kentucky's roads. Regional police, military checkpoints and roadside scenes.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]KI5 traffic.[/b] Replaces vanilla traffic, profession vehicles and roadside vehicle choices.
[*] [b]Crashed cars too.[/b] Replaces vanilla vehicle selections in crash scenes with KI5 vehicles.
[*] [b]Local police.[/b] Regional liveries for Louisville, Muldraugh, West Point and other jurisdictions.
[*] [b]Silent lightbars.[/b] Set the police lights chance from 0–100% in the ONLY KI5 sandbox page (default 50%). Eligible police spawns outside emergency parking flash with the siren off, in single-player and multiplayer.
[*] [b]Military encounters.[/b] Military zones, checkpoint fleets and four roadside stories with consistent camo.
[*] [b]Choose your military mix.[/b] Humvee spawn chance defaults to 95% of military cars/light vehicles. AM General military trucks defaults to 75% of the truck family mix. Both accept 0–100%; Humvee skin rarity stays separate.
[*] [b]Rare custom civilian skins.[/b] Custom civilian and novelty weight defaults to 0.025% of the original spawn weights in Sandbox Options > ONLY KI5. Set 0 to disable them or 100 for original weights. Police, military and emergency liveries stay enabled. This is a weight multiplier, not a fixed share of all cars.
[*] [b]Believable traffic.[/b] Louisville highway strongly favors ordinary cars. Limousines, street sweepers and other specialty vehicles are rare in general traffic.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[b]Build 42.21[/b], damnlib and More Car Features / WayMoreCars.

[b]No vehicle packs are required.[/b] Enable whichever KI5 vehicles fit your game; this mod automatically uses the enabled packs and skips missing cars and their skins. One pack, a selection, or the full fleet works. With no KI5 vehicles enabled, managed new-vehicle pools stay empty. No period-inappropriate car is forced on you.

Browse the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2490220997]KI5 collection[/url] or individual vehicle pages. The collection is a browsing link, not a complete requirements list. Load your chosen packs before this mod. Missing specialist packs leave those service/military roles empty; ordinary body styles can use other installed ordinary KI5 cars.

[b]YAPZLib is not required.[/b] Internal helpers replace it.

[b]Highly recommended:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3808100388]KI5 Vehicle Performance Patch (Unofficial)[/url] by lucygoosey. Optional; follow its server/client setup instructions.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Compatibility & saves[/h2]
Changes appear in [b]fresh chunks[/b]; existing vehicles are not rewritten. Back up saves before changing mods.

Disable standalone MCV, Consistent Camo, police skins/B42 hotfix, VMZ, No Vanilla selection mods and the old Vehicle Suite integrations: their selected content is already included.

Single-player, two-client multiplayer, silent lightbars and save/reload have been checked. Tested paths and known limits are recorded on GitHub.
