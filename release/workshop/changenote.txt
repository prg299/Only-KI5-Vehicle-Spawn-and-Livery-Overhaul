Only KI5 0.3.4 - choose your KI5 vehicle packs.

No vehicle packs are required. Enable whichever supported KI5 cars fit your game, including a small selection or a single pack. The Touran and other modern cars are optional. Only damnlib and More Car Features remain required. The collection is a browsing link rather than a complete installation list.

Missing vehicles and their integrated variants are excluded from map, profession, alias and military choices. Conditional variant initialization avoids references to missing templates/armor helpers. Compatible ordinary vehicles fill missing ordinary body styles; absent specialist families stay empty. With no KI5 packs enabled, all managed new-vehicle pools stay empty. Existing vehicles and the four spawn-setting defaults are unchanged.
